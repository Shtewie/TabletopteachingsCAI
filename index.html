What We Stand For

Handcrafted Worlds and Campaigns

Every adventure is written from scratch around the young heroes at the table for their interests, reading level, and the skills they're working on.

Fun and Safe Learning Environment

Clear table rules, gentle guidance and space for everyone to speak up. The learning happens because the game is genuinely fun.

No Experience or Equipment Required

Dice, character sheets, miniatures and rules know are all provided. Players can arrive having never rolled a d20 and be adventuring within minutes.

Custom Adventures

Each campaign and session is built around specific reading, writing and social objectives subtly imbued into the story

Take Home Educational Resources

Players leave with materials tied to the adventure. journals, reading passages, writing prompts and character work to continue between sessions.

Personalised Learning Records & Outcomes

Progress is tracked for each player and shared with families, so growth in literacy and social confidence is visible over time.

How To Join The Party
